Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is free forever, and the Pro trial runs for 14 days without asking for payment details.

How does the AI workflow builder work?

You describe a process in plain language. Flowly suggests steps, owners and timelines based on your team and past workflows. You review and edit everything before it runs.

Can I import work from other tools?

Yes. Import from CSV, Trello, Asana and Jira, or connect them so work keeps syncing both ways.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Business plans add SSO, audit logs and custom data retention.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the end of your billing period.
